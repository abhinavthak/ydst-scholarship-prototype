Good to know

Simple rules that keep the scholarship fair for everyone.

You're eligible if

  • You're a new applicant to an online program at one of the 18 partner universities
  • You meet the admission criteria of the program you choose
  • You haven't used this month's attempt yet
  • You enroll within 30 days of qualifying, for the current intake

Keep in mind

  • Can't be combined with any other discount or offer
  • Covers first-semester tuition only, not registration or exam fees
  • Valid only for the program you tested for, and not transferable
  • Unfair means flagged by proctoring void the scholarship
